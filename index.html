About DealerNexus AI

DealerNexus AI is a machine learning-powered dealership intelligence platform built for the South African automotive market.

The system leverages predictive analytics to estimate vehicle resale value, identify profitable opportunities, and optimize inventory decisions.

  • ✔ AI-driven price prediction
  • ✔ Profitability analysis
  • ✔ Sales speed estimation
  • ✔ Inventory intelligence

Our mission is to enable smarter, data-driven dealership decisions.
